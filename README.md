# 🚕 Gbera — The Future of Campus Transit

Welcome to **Gbera**, the exclusive, closed-campus ride-sharing platform designed specifically for students at the University of Ibadan.

Say goodbye to unpredictable fares, endless haggling, and unsafe ad hoc keke rides. Gbera is here to bring fast, flat-rate, and fully trackable mobility right to your fingertips.

---

## 🌟 What is Gbera?

Gbera is more than just an app; it's a movement to organize and elevate how we move around our campus. We replace the chaos of unstructured keke transit with a streamlined, reliable service matched by real campus geography, not generic city maps.

Whether you're rushing for a morning lecture at the Faculty of Science or heading back to Kuti Hall late at night, Gbera ensures you get there safely and fairly.

---

## ⚡ Why Choose Gbera?

- **💸 Flat-Rate Pricing**: No more price negotiations. Know exactly what you'll pay before you book, with transparent shared and exclusive ride options.
- **⏱️ Zero Wait Time Anxiety**: Our intelligent dispatch radar connects you with the nearest available vetted driver in seconds.
- **📍 True Campus Mapping**: We’ve stripped away the city clutter. Gbera’s maps are built around UI’s faculties, landmarks, and designated wait spots.
- **🛡️ Built-in Safety**: Only verified UI students can book rides. Share your live trip with a friend and access an instant SOS panic button right in the app.
